Final independent review found three Important adapter gaps: late disconnect
could dispatch another disconnect; a timed-out scan had no settlement path;
method payloads could falsely acknowledge success. Each was reproduced with
finite doubles, fixed, and regression-tested. Scan settlement participates in
Host shutdown evidence, and a subsequent explicit refresh releases the old
owner before obtaining fresh keys. Reply, cache and history retain one identical
cleanup attempt. No Critical or deferred Minor findings remained.

The finite scan-ingress test now waits for the scan reply before issuing shutdown;
immediate shutdown is allowed to supersede a pending read. Production scheduling
behavior was not changed for that test adjustment.

and every control AVAILABLE. Worker SDK release and successful exit were confirmed.

The existing App then scanned the controller and performed two read-only

AVAILABLE, and no native TLB child remained. The new GUI remains open.
